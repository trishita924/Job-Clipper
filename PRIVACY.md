# Privacy Policy — Job Clipper

**Job Clipper** respects your privacy and operates 100% locally on your device.

- **Local Storage Only**: All clipped job listings, application statuses, deadlines, personal notes, and reminder preferences are stored exclusively in your browser's `chrome.storage.local`.
- **No External Servers**: Job Clipper does not use any backend server, external database, analytics, or third-party tracking.
- **No Account Required**: You do not need to sign in or provide any personal credentials.
- **Page Access**: The content script reads job posting metadata from the active webpage **only** when you click **Save This Job** in the extension popup. It never bypasses login walls, CAPTCHAs, or restricted pages, and never transmits page data off your device.
